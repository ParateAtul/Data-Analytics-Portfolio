Zomato Restaurant Analysis
Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Zomato restaurant data to understand restaurant distribution, customer preferences, pricing patterns, ratings, and online food delivery trends.

Tools Used
Power BI

MySQL

Microsoft Excel

DAX

Dataset
Approximately 9,551 restaurant records.

Restaurant details, locations, cuisines, ratings, pricing, and service availability.

Key Analysis
Restaurant distribution across different countries and cities.

Restaurant openings by year, quarter, and month.

Analysis of customer ratings and price categories.

Table booking and online delivery trends.

Restaurant availability and geographical distribution.

Key Insights
Identified restaurant distribution patterns across different locations.

Analyzed customer ratings and pricing categories.

Evaluated online delivery and table booking preferences.

Explored restaurant growth trends over time.

Project Objective
To transform raw restaurant data into meaningful business insights that help understand restaurant performance, customer preferences, and market trends.
